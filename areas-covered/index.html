questions

Do you cover my address?+

Our advertised service area is within 18 miles of Darlington. Send your postcode so we can confirm travel availability for your address and service. If you are further afield, get in touch rather than assuming we cannot help.

Can I book automatically if I am inside the area?+

No. The current website prepares an enquiry for you to send through WhatsApp or email; it does not automatically check distance or reserve an appointment. We confirm the quote, location and availability directly.

Do you come to homes and workplaces?+

Yes. We are fully mobile and bring our own water and power. We discuss the location and whether it is suitable for the work before confirming the visit.

Can ceramic coating be done at my location?+

Ceramic coating requires suitable dry weather and curing conditions. Send your location and vehicle details so we can discuss a suitable arrangement.
