Common questions
about polishing

Can you remove every scratch?+

No. Some marks are too deep to remove safely. We first inspect the paint and discuss what can realistically be improved.

Do I need enhancement or correction?+

Enhancement focuses on lighter marks and gloss. Correction uses more intensive polishing for more noticeable swirls and marks where the paint allows. Send photos and tell us what concerns you if you would like help choosing.

Is preparation included?+

Yes. Paint Enhancement and Paint Correction include deep cleaning to remove stubborn contamination before machine polishing. Both include up to three months of paint protection.

Does this include a three-year ceramic coating?+

No. These polishing services include their stated short-term paint protection. The three-year ceramic coating is a separate package with its own preparation and single-stage machine polish included.

How do I arrange the work?+

Send your vehicle details, postcode and the result you want. We discuss the condition, agree the quote and confirm a suitable time and location. An enquiry does not automatically confirm an appointment.

Let’s talk about your paintwork.

Send a few photos and tell us which marks concern you. We’ll help you choose the right level of care, with the work and price agreed before booking.
