A few things
worth knowing.

Which clean should I choose?+

Choose an Essential Clean for regular upkeep, a Full Valet for deeper cleaning inside and out, or an Interior Deep Clean for the cabin only. If you are unsure, tell us about the condition and the result you want.

Will every stain or mark come out?+

Some stains and existing wear may remain. Send photos of the areas that concern you so we can discuss what is realistic before agreeing the work.

Can I arrange regular visits?+

Yes. Maintenance starts with an initial Full Valet, followed by upkeep based on an Essential Clean. Frequency, pricing and visit duration are agreed directly.

How do I arrange a visit?+

Send your vehicle details, postcode and the service you have in mind. We discuss the condition, confirm the quote and agree a suitable time and place. An enquiry does not automatically confirm an appointment.
