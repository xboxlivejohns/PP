What level of care does your vehicle need?

These are distinct services rather than inclusions in one package. If you are unsure, send a few photos with your enquiry and we can point you towards the most suitable starting point.

A practical refresh

Essential Clean / Mini Valet

For a car that needs a tidy, refreshed finish without the more intensive reset of a full valet. A sensible starting point when the vehicle is generally cared for but needs bringing back up to standard.

Best for: routine presentation and lighter cleaning needs.

The deeper reset

Full Valet

A more thorough clean inside and out for a deeper overall reset. The existing service information lists Deep Clean / Full Valet from £85, with typical appointments of 3–4 hours.

Best for: a vehicle needing a more complete clean.

Focused cabin care

Interior Deep Clean

Focused cleaning for built-up dirt and grime in the cabin. This is the right conversation when the interior is the main concern, rather than booking a full inside-and-out service by default.

Best for: tired, heavily used or visibly dirty interiors.
