Paint decontamination

What's That Purple Stuff on Your Paint?

Tiny iron particles can become embedded in vehicle paint, with brake dust being a major source. They aren't always visible until a specialist fallout remover reacts with them.

That pink and purple colour is the product working on iron contamination. Removing it leaves the paint cleaner and better prepared for further detailing.
