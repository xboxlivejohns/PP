Ask a question
Do you come to me?+

Yes. We’re fully mobile and bring our own water, power and professional equipment to your home or workplace. We’ll confirm the location and suitability when arranging your detail.

Which areas do you cover?+

Our advertised service area is within 18 miles of Darlington. Further afield? Get in touch to discuss your location and service. Send your postcode so we can confirm travel availability.

Which service is right for my car?+

Tell us what you want to improve and send a few photos. A valet cleans the car inside and out. Paint enhancement uses a single-stage machine polish for light marks. Paint correction uses more intensive polishing for deeper swirls where safe. Ceramic coating adds longer-term protection after the paint has been prepared.

Are the prices fixed?+

Services marked “From” are starting prices. Vehicle size and condition may affect the final quote. The work and price are agreed before your appointment.

What if the weather is unsuitable?+

Some work, especially ceramic coating, needs suitable dry weather and curing conditions. We’ll discuss conditions and confirm a suitable arrangement for your booking.
