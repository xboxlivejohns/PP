Paint protection

Ceramic Coating

The ceramic coating offered is Autosmart Matrix Blue, a three-year coating for prepared paintwork. The existing guide lists this service from £250 and approximately 5–7 hours including preparation, application and initial cure.

Only one coating duration is offered: three years.

Ask about ceramic coating

Wheel protection

Wheel Coating

A professional standalone coating service for wheels, helping protect the finish and making regular wheel care easier. Matrix Blue can also be used for wheels with two coats where this is the chosen approach.

Best for: customers who want dedicated protection for their wheels.

Ask about wheel coating

Exterior visibility care

Glass Protection

Professional protection for exterior glass, focused on helping water and contamination shed more easily. It is a standalone glass service rather than an automatic extra with paint coating.

Best for: exterior glass that you want easier to maintain.

Ask about glass protection

Film protection

PPF (Paint Protection Film)

A protective film option for selected painted areas of the vehicle. PPF is a different protection route from a ceramic coating, so tell us which areas you are considering when you enquire.

Best for: discussing protection for selected painted areas.
