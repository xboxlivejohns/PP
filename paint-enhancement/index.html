Paint enhancement & correction

More gloss, clarity and a cleaner finish.

Paintwork can need anything from a cleaner, glossier appearance to more intensive machine polishing. These services are separate, so the right choice starts with the condition of your paint.

The simple difference

Enhancement or correction?

Paint Enhancement is the lighter option: it is about improving gloss, clarity and the overall look. Paint Correction is more intensive machine polishing aimed at reducing visible swirls, scratches and other paint defects. It takes longer and depends more heavily on the paint's condition.

Gloss-led improvement

Paint Enhancement

A focused way to make paint look brighter, clearer and more refined. It suits paint that looks flat or tired when the main goal is a visible improvement in gloss rather than a full defect-removal process.

Best for: improving appearance without treating it as a full correction.

Paint Correction

Standalone specialist services

Preparation and smaller details matter too.

Decontamination and headlight restoration are individual services. They are not automatically included with Paint Enhancement or Paint Correction.

Cleaner paint surface

Paint Decontamination

Embedded or bonded contamination can leave paint feeling rough and stop it looking its best. Specialist fallout removal helps clean the surface and prepare it for further detailing.
