Website information

Your privacy.

This website is operated for Polished & Pristine Mobile, based in Darlington. For questions about your information, email polishedpristinemobile@gmail.com.

Preparing an enquiry

The quote builder prepares a message in your browser. It does not send your details to a website server or save them in browser storage. When you choose to open WhatsApp or email, your message is passed to that service so you can review and send it. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.

When you contact us

Information you choose to send, such as your name, vehicle, location and photographs, is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss a quote and arrange the requested service. Contact us if you want to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have shared. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies. It does not embed social media trackers, external fonts or third-party videos. The hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website.

External links

The Areas We Cover page offers an optional Google map. No Google Maps embed is requested until you select “Load Google map”. Loading it connects your browser to Google, which receives technical information such as your IP address and may use cookies under its own policies. You can hide the map again or use the external map link instead. See Google’s privacy information (opens in a new tab).

WhatsApp, telephone and email links open services outside this website. Check the relevant provider’s privacy information when using them.
