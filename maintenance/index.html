Maintenance Valet

The existing service guide lists Maintenance Valet from £45, with a typical visit of 1.5–2 hours for compact to mid-size vehicles. Pricing is confirmed for the vehicle and its condition before work begins.

  • Safe exterior pre-wash with snow foam
  • Two-bucket hand wash
  • Wheel deep clean
  • Tyre dressing
  • Interior vacuum
  • Dashboard and console wipe down
  • Glass cleaned inside and out

Important: Exterior washing is available only as part of regular, ongoing customer care. This is not advertised as a general one-off mobile wash service.
