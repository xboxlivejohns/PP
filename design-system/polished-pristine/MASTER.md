# Polished & Pristine — creative direction

## Identity
A hands-on automotive specialist whose real work carries the brand. The blue Porsche's paint, the emerald BMW and the reflective grey Range Rover supply the colour, texture and evidence. Condensed, confident typography gives the site a recognisable automotive voice; detailed photography keeps that voice personal and local.

The interlocked P/P emblem and stacked wordmark are custom vector artwork. Use the emblem in the header, footer and favicon. This is the proposed website identity, not an assertion that a pre-existing registered logo has changed.

## Composition
The homepage opens with the real blue Porsche, Finish obsessed typography and a small moving glimpse of a finished Mercedes. Mobile shows the recognisable front immediately after the headline, followed by 16px supporting text and the quote action. It uses content-driven height, with the image and action visible within a typical phone's first screen.

Three service pathways use tall, offset photographs rather than uniform service boxes. The ceramic story combines rotated vehicle and paint-detail photographs with a three-year protection seal. A native horizontal work track varies wide exterior and narrower interior images. The personal section brings the hands-on process back into view, followed by practical FAQs and a photo-led enquiry invitation.

The gallery opens with layered reflective paint photographs and uses a varied mosaic. Sixteen selected images include a white Vauxhall, blue BMW, Audi wheel detail and light-leather interior, bringing more variety to the original Porsche, green BMW and Range Rover selections. Finished imagery leads. The seat transformation explains its different viewpoints and lighting. A separate Range Rover story labels the initial paint assessment, machine preparation and finished view, with no aligned or quantified correction comparison.

Primary reading text uses 16px, supporting notes generally 14px and captions 12–14px. Large display type supplies the main identity; repeated tracked labels, photographic tilts and background words are reduced. PROTECT remains clearly visible at a smaller scale. The emphasis is finished work and the customer journey rather than repeated decorative treatments.

Inner pages retain the established journey and approved service structure while adopting the new typography, photographic treatment, identity and enquiry controls. Practical pricing and inclusions remain easy to scan.

## Motion
GSAP sequences the opening type and photograph, introduces the gallery's layered imagery and gently shifts one coating-detail photograph with scrolling. It does not animate every paragraph or take over scrolling. The portfolio track uses native horizontal scrolling, keyboard focus and explicit previous/next buttons.

The homepage finish film has visible play/pause controls, pauses offscreen and when the document is hidden, and defaults to a still under reduced motion or save-data. The gallery film is user initiated. CSS and GSAP honour reduced motion. All essential content is present without JavaScript.

## Applied specialist guidance
Frontend Design informed the subject-led identity, asymmetric photographic composition and deliberate type. UI/UX Pro Max informed responsive hierarchy, immersive imagery, clear enquiry paths and touch/keyboard access; its generic liquid-glass palette suggestions were rejected as unsuitable for this business. GSAP Core, Timeline, ScrollTrigger and Performance informed sequencing, transform-only animation and responsive motion. Vercel Web Interface Guidelines informed the final focus, media, form, keyboard, image-loading and overflow review.

No fabricated testimonials, correction percentages, review counts, opening hours or availability. Prices and package details remain sourced from the approved service JSON.
