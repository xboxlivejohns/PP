# Wash/dry/vacuum montage — research notes

This is a brief visual sequence, not a tutorial, service-inclusions list or claim that every service follows one fixed routine. The owner specifically removed machine polishing and its decontamination/preparation stages from the animation.

## Sequence and tools

| Moment | Recognisable tools |
| --- | --- |
| Vacuum | Canister vacuum, hose and crevice nozzle; door open |
| Wheels | Dedicated wheel-cleaning tools, brush and pressure rinse |
| Snow foam | Pressure washer, foam lance and bottle |
| Rinse | Pressure-washer lance without foam bottle |
| Contact wash | Shampoo bucket and a separate soft wash mitt |
| Final rinse | Pressure-washer lance |
| Dry | Large microfibre drying towel |
| Finish | Clean car, restrained glint; no polishing or coating action |

The vacuum appears first as an editorial choice. There is no universal rule that the interior must always come first: Auto Finesse explicitly allows interior cleaning before or after exterior work. Wheel tools are distinct from the body mitt. A wash mitt follows the pre-wash rinse; the final rinse comes before towel drying. Animation compresses product application, dwell times, multiple panels and tool changes; it is not a full operating procedure.

## Primary references

- [Auto Finesse — Complete Car Care Routine](https://www.autofinesse.com/blogs/car-detailing-guides/the-complete-car-care-routine): exterior order; contact wash and final rinse; separate, conditional preparation and polishing stages.
- [Auto Finesse — Detailing FAQs](https://www.autofinesse.com/pages/car-detailing-faqs): interior/exterior order can vary.

No products, equipment ownership, prices or business service inclusions were changed or inferred from these sources. The prototype contains generic tools, not a new product endorsement.
