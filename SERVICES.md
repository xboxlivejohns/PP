# Essential Clean

**Official name:** Essential Clean  
**Price:** From £45  
**Approximate duration:** 2 hours

## Purpose

A standard/basic maintenance valet for regular cleaning and upkeep.

It is not a deep interior clean, machine polishing or paint correction service.

## Exterior

- Exterior hand wash
- Snow foam/pre-wash
- Insect and bug residue removed as part of the exterior wash
- Wheels cleaned
- Tyres cleaned
- Exterior glass cleaned
- Vehicle dried
- Tyres dressed
- Door shuts cleaned
- High-SiO₂ spray ceramic sealant applied to the exterior to provide a rich, glossy finish

## Interior

- Professional vacuum throughout the interior
- Carpets professionally vacuumed
- Drill brushes used to agitate and lift dirt from carpets
- Specialist anti-static spray used during the professional vacuum process to freshen carpets
- Seats professionally vacuumed
- Mats cleaned
- Dashboard cleaned
- Interior plastics wiped down
- Interior glass cleaned
- General interior tidy-up
- Air freshener included

**The specialist anti-static spray is specific to Essential Clean and must not be added to Full Valet or Interior Deep Clean unless explicitly instructed.**

## Exclusions

- Deep interior cleaning
- Seat extraction
- Carpet extraction
- Machine polishing
- Paint correction
- Ceramic Coating
- Headlight Restoration
- Paint Decontamination
- Pet hair removal

# Paint Decontamination

**Official name:** Paint Decontamination  
**Price:** From £60

## Purpose

A standalone service to remove bonded and embedded contamination from exterior paintwork, leaving the surface clean and smooth.

It prepares paintwork for machine polishing or protection.

## Contamination removed

Different types of contamination require their own appropriate product and process.

The process can address:

- Tar
- Road grease and grime
- Bird lime
- Mould
- Iron fallout

A clay bar is then used to remove remaining bonded contamination.

## Relationship to other services

Paint Decontamination is the required first step for:

- Paint Enhancement
- Paint Correction
- Ceramic Coating

When it forms part of one of those services, it is **included in the service price**.

The customer must not be instructed to book or pay for Paint Decontamination separately when it is already required as part of Paint Enhancement, Paint Correction or Ceramic Coating.

**Duration has not been confirmed. Do not invent one.**

# Paint Enhancement

**Official name:** Paint Enhancement  
**Price:** £170  
**Approximate duration:** 3–4 hours

## Purpose

For tired, dull or lacklustre paintwork where the main aim is to restore gloss, clarity and depth rather than carry out intensive defect removal.

## Included

1. Paint Decontamination, required first and included in the price
2. Machine polishing
3. Fine polishing pad and finishing polish
4. Refinement of the clear coat/paintwork
5. Restoration of gloss, clarity and depth
6. Focus on lighter surface defects and overall finish refinement
7. Up-to-3-month paint protection applied after machine polishing

## Result

The aim is a highly glossy, refined, near mirror-like finish.

## Important distinction

Paint Enhancement is not intensive paint correction and must not be described as a multi-stage correction package.

# Paint Correction

**Official name:** Paint Correction  
**Price:** £200  
**Approximate duration:** Approximately 5 hours / most of a day, subject to vehicle size and condition

## Purpose

Machine polishing and correction for paintwork with swirl marks and suitable paint defects.

## Included

1. Paint Decontamination, required first and included in the price
2. Machine polishing/correction
3. Correction of approximately **95% of swirl marks and paint defects that can be safely corrected within the clear coat**
4. Fine polishing/finishing stage where required
5. Up-to-3-month paint protection applied after correction

## Scratches and defects

Scratches remaining within the clear coat can generally be corrected through machine polishing.

Deeper scratches that extend beyond the clear coat may not be safely correctable.

Anything requiring sanding and colour-matched paint repair is outside this service.

**Do not promise perfect or 100% correction.**

# Ceramic Coating

**Official name:** Ceramic Coating  
**Coating duration:** 3 years  
**Price:** From £250, depending on vehicle size  
**Approximate duration:** Approximately one full day

## Important

There is only **one Ceramic Coating service**.

It is a **3-year ceramic coating**.

Do not create 1-year, 2-year, 5-year, tiered or packaged ceramic coating options.

## Required preparation — included in the price

1. Paint Decontamination
2. Fallout removal
3. Cleaning and preparation
4. Machine polishing

Paint Decontamination and machine polishing are included in the Ceramic Coating price. Customers do not book or pay for them separately.

## Included protection

- 3-year ceramic coating
- Wheel face coating
- All exterior glass coating

## Machine polishing

Machine polishing improves:

- Gloss
- Clarity
- Finish
- Suitable swirls and paint defects

The final result depends on the condition of the paintwork.

## Wheel face coating

Wheel coating is included with Ceramic Coating only.

- Wheels remain fitted to the vehicle
- Wheel faces only
- Decontamination
- Fallout removal
- Cleaning and degreasing
- 3-year coating

**Wheel barrels are not included.**

Wheel coating is not a standalone service and has no separate price.

Never imply that wheels are removed.

## Exterior glass coating

All exterior glass is coated as part of Ceramic Coating.

Glass coating is not a standalone service and has no separate price.

## Benefits

- Deep gloss
- Highly reflective, mirror-like finish
- Water beading
- Hydrophobic protection
- Easier maintenance
- UV protection
- Long-lasting protection

The coating is rated for 3 years, with protective benefits intended to last for years when properly maintained.

Dark vehicles can show particularly impressive results, depending on paint condition.

## Important distinction

The high-SiO₂ spray ceramic sealant used in Essential Clean and Full Valet is **not** this 3-year Ceramic Coating.

# Headlight Restoration

**Official name:** Headlight Restoration  
**Price:** £60  
**Approximate duration:** 1–2 hours

## Purpose

Restores the clarity and appearance of faded, cloudy or weathered headlights.

## Process

### 1. Specialist cleaning

Headlights are thoroughly cleaned with specialist cleaning chemicals to remove surface contamination.

### 2. Wet sanding

Progressively coarse-to-fine grades are used to remove oxidation and surface deterioration and restore clarity.

### 3. Two-stage machine polishing

After sanding, the headlights receive a two-stage machine polish progressing from coarse to fine.

This further refines the surface and improves clarity.

### 4. Protection

The restored headlights are protected with a **3-year ceramic coating**.
